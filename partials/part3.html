Transformative Learning Framework

Engagement for All

Learning Engagement Indicators describe what students experience, do, and become.

Facet 1

Personalisation

Learning responds to students’ identities, strengths, needs, interests, cultures, languages, and aspirations.

1.4 · Students demonstrate understanding through multiple languages, modes, and pathways.
Facet 2

Agency

Students take ownership by making meaningful decisions, reflecting on growth, and influencing their future.

2.2 · Decisions about what, how, and how understanding is demonstrated.
Facet 3

Authenticity

Learning connects to meaningful real-world contexts, challenges, audiences, and purposes.

3.1 · Work reflects authentic practices, audiences, and purposes beyond school.
Facet 4

Creativity

Learners generate ideas, innovate, solve problems, take thoughtful risks, and express thinking in original ways.

4.1 · Students generate and contribute original ideas, products, and solutions.
Facet 5

Taking Action

Students apply learning to make informed, purposeful, and ethical contributions.

5.1 · Students apply learning to authentic opportunities and challenges.
Facet 6

Collaboration

Learners work purposefully and respectfully with others to build understanding and solve complex problems.

6.1 · Students collaborate purposefully to deepen learning and achieve shared goals.
Teacher Growth & Reflection

Four interconnected domains

Growth is developmental, evidence-informed, and anchored in student experience.

Domain 1

Purposeful & Inclusive Learning Design

Learning purpose, understanding learners, inclusive engagement/materials, and assessment planning for equity and growth.

Domain 2

Inclusive Learning Culture & Environment

Belonging, student voice, equitable routines, and purposeful use of space, time, and tools.

Domain 3

Transformative, Culturally Responsive Learning in Action

Cognitive demand, inclusive discourse, choice/inquiry/creativity, and authentic application/social impact.

Domain 4

Collaborative Planning, Reflection & Professional Impact

Feedback, evidence/data, learner reflection, collaboration, and community partnership.
