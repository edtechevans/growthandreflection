Unit reflection · Grade 8

How much agency is really here?

“Students can choose how they present their learning, but I am not sure they are making decisions that actually shape the inquiry.”

AI reflection · demo

Your unit already gives students meaningful choice in how they communicate understanding. The clearest opportunity is to move some of that choice earlier — from selecting a product to making decisions that shape the question, pathway, or evidence they pursue.

What I notice

“Students may demonstrate their final understanding through a podcast, public-information infographic, or prototype. All groups investigate the same teacher-selected sustainability question.”
EvidenceUnit overview · final performance task

There is authentic choice in expression, but the intellectual direction of the inquiry remains largely teacher-defined. That distinction matters when thinking about agency: students are choosing how to communicate, but have fewer opportunities to influence what they investigate.
