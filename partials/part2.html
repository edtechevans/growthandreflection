MTSS / Tier 1

Design access before adding intervention

A strong Tier 1 response anticipates learner variability and adjusts the core learning experience before interpreting difficulty as a need for more intensive support.

Teacher Growth Continuum

3c. Choice, Inquiry & Creativity

The continuum moves from teacher-prescribed pathways toward students generating ideas, making decisions, independently managing inquiry, and ultimately designing learning pathways.

Source: Teacher Growth & Reflection Framework · Domain 3c, pp. 20–21

What this suggests together

The next move is not “more choice” in the abstract. It is to preserve clarity and access while shifting a consequential decision to students. That simultaneously strengthens agency, UDL-aligned design, inclusive Tier 1, and the student experience described in the Growth Continuum.
